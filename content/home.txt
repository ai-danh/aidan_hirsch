TITLE: About Me 
PHOTO:
BODY: I'm a Biomedical Engineering student at Purdue with research focus on BCI applications for nervous system injury recovery. 

Currently, I'm developing a behavior study to encode valence as signed spatial information in ICMS patterns in mouse S1/M1 cortices to investigate human proprioceptive control of bidirectional neuroprostheses.

In my previous research, I've trained a DeepLabCut pose estimation model for mouse forelimb motor cortex analysis and worked on improving ultramicroelectrode longevity in rats in-vivo using equivalent circuit modeling derived from EIS data. My technical tools include DeepLabCut, CEBRA, impedance spectroscopy, cyclic voltammetry, and PCB design through KiCAD and LTSpice.
